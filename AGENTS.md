# Repository working instructions

Work only in this standalone editor repository. Do not clone, read, edit or depend
on the engine repository. Engine requirements belong in docs/ENGINE_NEEDS.md;
Joshua decides future integration and merges. Keep work private and costs unapproved.

Read docs/STATUS.md and docs/DELIVERY_CHECKLIST.md before continuing work.
After each task, update both documents. Tick checklist steps only after their
complete outcomes are implemented and appropriately verified; partial steps stay
unchecked with progress/evidence notes. Preserve checklist IDs, keep the completion
count/current position accurate and distinguish local implementation from review
or merge. See the checklist's maintenance rules and docs/REVIEW_STACK.md.
